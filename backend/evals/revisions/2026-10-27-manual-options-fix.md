### 3. Considering only the tail-packing effect described, which change explains membrane parts moving more freely?

- a. More saturated tails, which pack closely and allow less movement.
- b. More saturated tails, which pack loosely and allow more movement.
- c. More unsaturated tails, which pack closely and allow less movement.
- d. More unsaturated tails, which pack loosely and allow more movement.

Correct: **d**. Unsaturated tails with double-bond bends pack loosely, letting membrane parts move more and increasing fluidity.

### 4. A student says cholesterol always makes a membrane more fluid. Which correction fits the explanation?

- a. It limits movement in a very fluid membrane and promotes stiffening in the cold.
- b. It increases movement in a very fluid membrane and prevents stiffening in the cold.
- c. It limits movement in a very fluid membrane and prevents stiffening in the cold.
- d. It increases movement in a very fluid membrane and promotes stiffening in the cold.

Correct: **c**. Cholesterol buffers fluidity: it limits movement when the membrane is too fluid and helps prevent stiffening when cold.
