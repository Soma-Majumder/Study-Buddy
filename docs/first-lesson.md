## Proposed source passage

Adapted from the selected NASA learning objectives and discussion:

In photosynthesis, plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to produce glucose, a sugar used as food, and release oxygen. Sunlight supplies energy for this process. Water and carbon dioxide are inputs; glucose and oxygen are products. The lesson asks students to predict how limiting sunlight, water, or carbon dioxide could affect a plant. This introductory lesson focuses on these requirements and products.
