Study Buddy: what our evaluations show

The controller coordinates the study workflow and enforces stopping rules. Live handoffs were accepted in the saved sample, but Assessment’s automatic quiz revision did not recover an approved quiz in the six observed attempts.

No single overall “agent score.” These results measure different things. Fixture success, historical live-output comparisons, replayed content and manual corrections must not be combined into one accuracy percentage. Preparing this report made no paid calls.

Manual corrections: separate demonstration evidence

The manually corrected first quiz and retry each passed a paid Reviewer check. The first quiz retained style warnings; the retry was classified as eight understanding questions with a repeated-target warning.

A later isolated completion test replayed approved saved quizzes, saved first feedback and saved reteaching, then graded a simulated retry at 10/10 after the saved 7/10 first attempt. One live retry-feedback call passed validation. The controller saved one final summary, marked the session complete, and the summary was verified after reopening the database. Exactly one learning retry was used, with zero foreign-key violations.

This is staged, human-assisted verification—not an uninterrupted fully live success. The simulated scores are not evidence of learning effectiveness. The failed earlier session was not revived or its failure overridden.

Cheap option-balance diagnostic

The private check flags a correct option when its character length exceeds 1.3 × the mean length of the other three choices. All 14 fixture cases produced flags, commonly Q9; the cases contain only 8 unique candidate snapshots.

These are heuristic warnings, not proof of answer clues or weak distractors. No flags would not establish quality. The check runs offline only; it has not been added to runtime dispatch or granted another revision allowance. No answer letters, option text or keys appear in these aggregate reports.

Conclusion and assignment boundary

Verified: fixture coordination, observed saved handoff acceptance, bounded failures, deterministic grading, and persistence through summary using approved saved quizzes.

Unresolved: fresh automatic quiz generation and its one revision have not demonstrated reliable Reviewer acceptance. The latest fresh run retained weak distractors in Q3 and answer clues in Q4; the controller withheld publication.

Paid tuning is stopped. Preparing this report required no extra agents, expanded review loops, weakened gates, frontend changes or paid calls. Any future paid evaluation needs renewed authorization.
